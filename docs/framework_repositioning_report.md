# RDHO Framework Repositioning Report

## Decision and scope

The manuscript is repositioned as **an RDHO-based capacity-feasible joint task-offloading and computing-resource-allocation framework, accompanied by controlled evidence that separates the complete pipeline effect from the isolated population-update effect**.

The approved paper title is:

> RDHO-Based Capacity-Feasible Joint Task Offloading and Computing Resource Allocation in Mobile Edge Computing: A Controlled Evaluation

RDHO remains the paper's featured solver and the primary object of method design and evaluation. This framing is justified because the implemented RDHO workflow integrates legal-node encoding, physical-resource decoding, deterministic capacity repair, RIME-DBO-inspired population updates, dynamic search guidance, fixed-objective incumbent tracking and optional coordinate refinement. The controlled evidence changes the supported interpretation of the population update; it does not justify deleting RDHO or changing the model.

## RDHO is front-loaded, with a bounded claim

- The title, abstract early method sentence, post-gap Introduction paragraph, second contribution, Section 5 title, Algorithm 1, Section 6 comparison structure and conclusion name RDHO explicitly.
- Section 5 retains the RDHO formulas and pseudocode. It describes RIME-inspired and DBO-inspired moves as components, without assuming that their fusion is inherently stronger.
- The end-to-end result remains, but is explicitly labeled a **configured end-to-end solver comparison with unequal NFE**. It evaluates the complete procedures and is not used as proof that the population operator alone is superior.
- The old wording that implied a universally superior hybrid, an optimal exploration-exploitation balance, superiority over both parents, or that the hybrid operator was the primary cause of improvement was removed or replaced with this integrated-framework interpretation.

## What the manuscript can and cannot claim

It can claim that RDHO is the featured, capacity-feasible integrated framework; that the configured RDHO-full end-to-end procedure has the lowest reporting fitness in the frozen V2 comparison; that RDHO improves on RIME under both strict controls; and that the study provides empirical evidence about attribution confounders in metaheuristic evaluation.

It cannot claim that the RIME-DBO population update is independently superior to DBO, universally superior, an optimal fusion, or validated as the cause of the configured end-to-end advantage. DBO's controlled advantage is stated in the abstract, Section 6, Table 7, Figure 12 and conclusion.
