# Manuscript Impact Report: Controlled RDHO Evidence

No manuscript source, DOCX, table, or figure was modified in this task.

The new controlled evidence does not support a claim that the RDHO population
mechanism is independently superior to both implemented parent algorithms.
With shared initial populations, common decoder and repair, an identical fixed
reporting objective, and equal total NFE, RDHO is significantly better than
RIME but significantly worse than DBO in both the population-stage-only and
common-refinement experiments.

The following conclusion is supported for author review:

> The controlled experiment does not support independent superiority of the
> RDHO population mechanism. In the studied V2 scenarios, the implemented RDHO
> update outperformed implemented RIME but was significantly outperformed by
> implemented DBO under both population-stage-only and common-refinement
> controls.

Do not revise the manuscript to assert a general or independent population
mechanism advantage. Existing end-to-end claims should not be expanded from
these results. Any manuscript change remains an author decision after review
of the saved raw data and statistics.

`results/statistics/controlled_evidence_effect_sizes.csv`, and the raw result
